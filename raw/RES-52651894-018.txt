About us
We are here to help foster businesses from their earliest stages to their expansion. Be it just an early-stage venture with a few months of operations or innovative advanced business, we want to support it. Successful businesses are often started by mature founders who have several years of work experience and do not conform to the status quo.
That’s why we are looking for professionals or ambitious talents who have a knack for solving market failures and those who embody leadership and efficient problem-solving qualities.
The qualities that make a great entrepreneur are often present before they even start a business. We are looking for extraordinary founders to partner with them from the early days of starting up as well as partner in theirs already grown innovative business ideas. We want to cooperate with hungry entrepreneurs, willing to take a leap of faith in your business venture & success.
We provide a new dimension of start-up ecosystem with unique DNA to boost any innovative businesses through our 0100 Ventures holding. Together with funding we organize key European venture capital & start-up conferences and provide software development resources as well as co-working spaces and transaction advisory services .

Early Stage
“Pre-Seed”
Parameters
Strong founding team
Strong idea which is properly planned
Detailed product roadmap and business plan
Technical team or resources ensuring the R&D
Meeting ZGC fund’s investment criteria
Benefits
Up to 200k € intro investment
Active mentoring, strategic and support from ZGC
Access to ZGC perks package (AWS, …)
Talent acquisition support via Campus, 0100 and ZCG
Networking, support and synergies from ZGC companies
Continuation of follow-on investments up to S-A
Relationship building with our business partners for scale-up
Later Stage
“Seed / Early S-A”
Parameters
Fully formed experienced founding team
End-to-end business planned
At least MVP commercially launched
Initial business traction & revenues
Scaling of a business pending
Meeting ZGC fund’s investment criteria
Benefits
All Pre-Seed benefits
Lead or syndicate financing to cover your needs up to X M EUR
Active fostering of your business venture by ZGC partners
Help to create top-notch advisory, technical & supervisory boards
Active support for your follow-on funding via VC/PE conferences
Expectations
Competitive Advantage
Capability to develop globally viable market strategy and competitive edge
Room for Growth & Expansion
Innovative and scalable business model with potential to expand through new geographies and markets
Fresh ideas/No recycling
Up-to 3/5 years ideas, products & entities w/o previous spin-offs, transfers of IP, or pivoted previous products/businesses
Companies without vast public funding
Companies without involvement of public funding such as Horizon 2020 or other larger EU grant schemes
Full focus & Commitment
Founders ready to commit 99% of their time and focus on our business venture/invested business
Innovative businesses
At least 10% of expenditures spent on research & development (incl. IT development), and industry experts determine the idea as innovative
Early stage readiness
Strong experienced founding team, At least first MVP in R&D, Business idea fully formed, GTM planned, Launch ready
Non-adjacent product to businesses of your relatives
Relatives of key founders (over 25% shareholders) are not working on adjacent and/or connected products and businesses as the founders
Slovak entity/teams with international ambition
Slovak Non-Bratislava or Bratislava based teams & operations with ambition to go abroad and scale up
Good track-record of businesses & founders
No previous tax, legal, employment, AML, KYC claims from the authorities. Business without debts, firm not in difficulty (no negative net assets)
No negative impact industries
Businesses not operating in: Weapons, Gambling, Drugs, Tobacco, Alcohol, Mining, Pornography, Illegal activities.
No conflict of interest
Key founders (over 25% shareholders) shall not be involved in other illegible businesses (over 25% shareholding in other 5+ years old businesses)

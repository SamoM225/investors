SRKF has provided a convertible loan to Ecocapsule
July 2018 &mdash; Slovenský rastový kapitálový fond, a.s. (SRKF) has participated in a fundraising round with Ecocapsule Holding, a.s. via a convertible loan.
SRKF is a government-sponsored investment fund focused on equity investments for small an medium-sized companies.
Based in Bratislava, Slovakia, Ecocapsule designs, develops and manufactures smart, self-sustainable micro-homes, which use solar and wind energy. Customers can stay in remote places out of reach of infrastructure, with the luxury of a hotel room. Ecocapsule is a design way for independent housing. It can serve as a cottage, pop-up hotel, caravan, mobile office or research station.
Ecocapsule Holding is the actual operating entity behind Ecocapsule. The holding initiated a fundraising round in order to finance Ecocapsule's further expansion.
Our role in the transaction
Oaklins' team in Slovakia served as a financial advisor to SRKF in this transaction.

Comtax has been acquired by Ryan
Comtax, a São Paulo-based tax technology consultancy specializing in tax determination and digital tax compliance, had been acquired by Ryan, the world’s largest firm dedicated exclusively to business taxes. The transaction marks Ryan’s entry into the Brazilian market, adding a specialized team and an established base of large corporate clients in Brazil, one of the world’s most complex tax environments, at a time when the country’s consumption tax reform is set to reshape how companies calculate and report indirect taxes. For Comtax, the acquisition brings access to Ryan’s global platform, technology and client network across more than 80 countries, creating opportunities to scale its tax determination and compliance offering beyond its current footprint. Comtax’s founders will continue to lead the business.

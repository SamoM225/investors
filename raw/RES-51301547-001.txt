FIRST THING YOU SHOULD KNOW About Us Financing is only the beginning We are a platform focused on providing financing and support to seed-stage fintech startups. We are continuously building a strong fintech network in Europe to leverage for our portfolio companies. We also provide hands-on operational and strategic support to our startups - from pitch deck reviews to running strategic workshops; we help our startups when and where needed. Our Thesis: We Get Banking Our goal is to create an ecosystem for knowledge sharing and potential synergies between our startups, partners and the banking sector as a whole. We understand how the incumbents operate and we are fully aware of intricacies of financial services such as regulation and other challenges, which startups typically face. Our Thesis: Our Focus in on European Fintech We are based in CEE but we are investing anywhere in Europe where we see outstanding nascent fintech startups - from London to Riga. Our ultimate goal is to support our companies in scaling across the continent and globally. Who Are We Looking For? We invest in and around seed stage in Europe. Our primary focus is on b2b and b2b2c fintech startups disrupting legacy infrastructure and distribution models and creating value for end consumers and SMEs. We are looking for ambitious team with unique technology-drive solutions, drive to scale, smart approach to distribution and vision to change financial services for the better. If this sounds like you don't be shy and send us your pitch deck. We understand that fundraising is time and effort-intensive process for founders. We strive to be as founder-friendly as possible and cut down the fundraising time to weeks.

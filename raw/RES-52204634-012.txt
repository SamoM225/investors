IPM Avanea Eco Management
From resources managed under the auspices of NDF II., in June 2019 SIH supported investments in environmental projects in Slovakia in collaboration with financial intermediary – IPM Avanea Eco Management a.s.
IPM Avanea Eco Management is a member of the IPM Group and co-investor in the fund, which manages the waste management fund through which prospective companies will be sought and funded. The main goal is to increase the market capacity for sorting waste, along with its evaluation and recycling.

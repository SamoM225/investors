What do we offer?
Growth capital
Eterus is a partner for financing growth-stage companies with capable management and a proven concept.
Minority position of the fund
The fund enters companies as a minority shareholder and management control remains in the hands of the original owners.
Equity investments
We are an equity fund investing in shares and other equity instruments of companies domiciled in Slovakia. We do not offer debt financing.
Investment opportunities and horizon
We target tickets in the range of EUR 2–2.5 million, with a preferable investment duration of 5-7 years.

How do we operate, and what can we bring to the table?
Transparent and efficient entry process
01
Our local team is responsible for managing the transaction, ensuring fast and efficient investment process from the start of discussions to the signing of the contract documentation.
Once the necessary opportunity analysis and due diligence of the target company have been carried out, the Investment Committee's decision can be implemented in a very short time.
Fund as a partner
02
By strengthening the company's capital structure, the fund opens up new financing opportunities through improved credibility.
In general, the presence of an established financial investor in the shareholder structure increases trustworthiness with both financing institutions, as well as with potential future strategic partners and investors.
Increasing business credibility
03
By strengthening the company's capital structure, the fund opens up new financing opportunities through improved credibility.
In general, the presence of an established financial investor in the shareholder structure increases trustworthiness with both financing institutions, as well as with potential future strategic partners and investors.
Post-transaction support
04
Eterus representatives can assist in financial and strategic areas of the business, add value in debt financing negotiations, find optimal solutions for new project funding structures, or in financial restructuring.
Fund representatives often act as advisors to the CEO/Partner and offer an independent view on strategic topics for the company.
The fund can also identify potential acquisition targets, negotiate takeover deals, structure the financing of these opportunities, and ensure the company's sustained growth as a market consolidator.

SFDR DISCLOSURE.

Disclosures under Regulation (EU) 2019/2088 on sustainability-related disclosures in the financial services sector (SFDR).

This disclosure is provided by Hilberman Capital, s.r.o., with its registered office at Pribinova 30, 821 09 Bratislava, ID: 53602650 (the "Registered Manager"), registered in the Register of Alternative Investment Fund Managers maintained by the National Bank of Slovakia under registration number 0049.

The disclosure is available on the company's website for the purpose of complying with transparency requirements under Regulation (EU) 2019/2088 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 November 2019 on sustainability-related disclosures in the financial services sector (SFDR) and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/1288.

This disclosure is made at entity level in relation to the alternative investment fund managed by the Registered Manager:

Hilberman Capital Fund I, s.r.o., with its registered office at Pribinova 17/30, 821 09 Bratislava, ID: 56342900 (the "Fund").

1. INTEGRATION OF SUSTAINABILITY RISKS
Article 3(1) SFDR

Sustainability risks, being environmental, social or governance events or conditions that, if they occur, could cause an actual or potential material adverse impact on the value of an investment, are considered by the Registered Manager as part of its investment decision-making process.

The Registered Manager integrates sustainability risks into:

pre-investment screening and due diligence procedures,
investment analysis and internal approval processes,
risk documentation and monitoring throughout the holding period.

As part of its investment process, the Registered Manager assesses relevant ESG factors proportionate to the nature of the target investment, its sector and transaction structure. Identified sustainability risks are evaluated for their materiality and may influence the investment decision.

Where appropriate, the Registered Manager may decide not to proceed with an investment or may implement mitigation measures designed to reduce identified sustainability risks.

The integration of sustainability risks is subject to the principle of proportionality, taking into account the size, nature and complexity of the Fund's activities.

2. PRINCIPAL ADVERSE IMPACTS ON SUSTAINABILITY FACTORS
Article 4(1) SFDR

The Registered Manager currently does not consider principal adverse impacts of investment decisions on sustainability factors within the meaning of Article 4(1)(b) SFDR.

While sustainability risks are integrated into the investment decision-making process, the Registered Manager has determined that, taking into account the size of the organisation, the nature of its investment strategy and the availability of reliable data from portfolio companies, it is not currently in a position to systematically collect and report all data required under the principal adverse impact regime in a consistent and cost-effective manner.

The administrative burden and data limitations associated with full implementation of the principal adverse impact reporting framework are currently considered disproportionate in the context of the Fund's investment strategy.

This position will be kept under review, particularly in light of regulatory developments, evolving market practice and increased data availability.

3. REMUNERATION POLICY AND SUSTAINABILITY RISKS
Article 5(1) SFDR

The Registered Manager's remuneration policy is designed to promote sound and effective risk management and does not encourage excessive risk-taking that would be inconsistent with the Fund's risk profile or investment strategy.

The remuneration structure consists of fixed and, where applicable, variable components. The variable component is linked to long-term performance of the Fund and reflects overall risk management considerations, including the proper identification and management of sustainability risks.

The remuneration framework is aligned with the integration of sustainability risks into the investment decision-making process and supports a long-term value creation approach.

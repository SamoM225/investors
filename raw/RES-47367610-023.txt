About
BiteBerry combines food industry experience with AI and automation to create tools for improving restaurant efficiency and profitability. Their primary product is a voice ordering system that automates phone orders, integrating with existing point-of-sale systems. This technology aims to reduce reliance on third-party delivery platforms and their associated fees.

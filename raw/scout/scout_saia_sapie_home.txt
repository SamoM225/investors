 Impact 
 SAPIE is an independent and non-profit platform whose mission is to empower the Slovak innovation ecosystem. It's the largest professional association representing almost 200 member companies and organizations. 
 SAPIE is a leading platform for policy debate on digital economy and innovation in Slovakia aspiring to unite leaders within the CEE region. It is active in forming collaborative networks of private and public actors, supporting the startup scene and community and enhancing digital transformation of SMEs within the CEE region.

 Michal Kardoš 
 CEO of SAPIE
 We connect and represent almost 200 innovative companies in Slovakia. SAPIE membership offers you various benefits, including  opportunities for networking with people from innovative companies, inspiration from fellow members during our events, participation in our projects or foreign missions all around the globe.
 Join our vibrant community today! We invite you to be a part of something special. Connect with us and let's grow together.
 For us, SAPIE doesn't replace the way we already build relationships, it adds a layer on top of it. Rooms and conversations we wouldn't otherwise be part of. That means fewer dead ends and more direct paths to the right people and the kind of innovative companies we actually want to work with. The foreign missions and business networking are the benefits we value the most. SAPIE membership has given us more contacts, stronger relationships, more visibility, and real opportunities to grow the business. 
 Michal Rusko Chief Business Officer and Partner, Touch4IT

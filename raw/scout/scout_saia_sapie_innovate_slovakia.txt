 Innovate Slovakia
 Empowering the Slovak innovation and startup scene by providing reliable data on the innovation ecosystem.
 Information and data are critical for fact-based decision making and the growth of the startup and innovation ecosystem. Innovate Slovakia’s mission is to collect and provide reliable data on the innovation ecosystem of Slovakia. By collecting data on startups, scaleups, investors, accelerators and investment rounds, it helps entrepreneurs orient themselves in this environment. Moreover, it showcases the potential of Slovakia as the birthplace of many successful companies. 
 Objectives 
 To help the Slovak innovation and startup ecosystem flourish, we decided to create a place that will help key stakeholders find all opportunities and use them to the maximum. By doing so, we hope to become a catalyst for better and more innovative Slovakia.
 This initiative was created as a collaboration of an innovation consultancy Civitta and SAPIE – Slovak Alliance for Innovation Economy.
 Success stories 
 We curated a list of top Slovak startups to watch in 2023 based on traction, investment raised, and potential on the market. To inspire new entrepreneurs we also put together a list of “success stories” – these are Slovak tech companies that grown into mature companies with significant sales and employment or have been acquired. 
 Our ambition is not to provide an objective list of companies but to showcase a selection of startups tech companies to illustrate the growth of the Slovak startup ecosystem. If you think there is someone missing, do let us know!

 Tomáš Bél
 Partner 
 Tomáš is an Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of 2010 in Slovakia. He is also a serial entrepreneur with many successful projects. 
 His strength is orientation and experience in many different areas and business sectors. As a result, he has a synthetic view of things and their generalisation, which helps identify new business opportunities. 
 During his career, he has gained hands-on experience in almost all areas of company management. Recently, he has been working on technology and digital innovation. 
 Tomáš is also active in the startup community in Slovakia, both as a mentor and investor but also as an active participant in various startup competitions. He completed several study stays abroad, such as Harvard Business School, which gave him a strong global view of business and valuable networking, or study stays in investment and corporate finance at London Business School. He is a member of the Harvard Business Alumni Club.
 We seek and actively connect ideas, people and capital for the exponential growth of a company. Vision Ventures is a venture capital investor. We support ideas or companies at the beginning of their journey.

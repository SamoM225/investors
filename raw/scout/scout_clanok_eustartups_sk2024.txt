 10 Slovakian startups to watch in 2024 and beyond!
 By Tea Vrcic 
 March 19, 2024 
 Share
 Facebook
 Twitter
 WhatsApp
 Linkedin
 While Slovakia has placed poorly in previous international rankings for innovation capacity, it is still home to a range of innovators developing groundbreaking solutions and startups addressing global challenges, some of which are attracting international attention. 
 Slovakia’s strategic location offers easy access to Vienna and neighbouring markets like Czech Republic, Ukraine, and Poland, making it an attractive destination for startups seeking diverse market opportunities. Political stability further enhances its appeal as a conducive environment for startup growth and investment. 
 A vibrant startup community thrives in Bratislava, complemented by emerging hubs near technical universities in Zilina and Kosice. These ecosystems span various sectors, including healthcare, fintech, and mobility, fostering innovation and entrepreneurship. The Slovak Alliance for Innovation Economy (SAPIE) plays a crucial role in promoting entrepreneurship through initiatives like Innovate Slovakia. 
 While the government collaborates with SAPIE to support startups, regulatory improvements and enhanced public funding policies are still needed. Positive steps, such as tailored legislation and tax incentives, have been taken, but challenges remain, including the shortage of IT talent. 
 Slovakia awaits its first unicorn startup but has seen notable early exits, signalling potential for growth. In the following list, we are putting a spotlight on the 10 promising Slovakian startups, all founded post-2021, showcasing the rising potential and innovation within Slovakia’s dynamic startup ecosystem: 
AUGLIO: Bratislava-based AUGLIO developed an AR/Virtual Mirror SaaS platform, seamlessly blending the physical and virtual realms to revolutionise online shopping experiences. Founded in 2022, Auglio created 3D products from 2D images and facilitates immersive online try-ons. The startup raised €1.6 million to boost conversion rates for thousands of global e-commerce businesses. 
 Ayanza: 
 Bratislava-based Ayanza is revolutionising team collaboration for remote and hybrid work environments. Founded in 2021, Ayanza empowers organisations to streamline workflows, foster effective communication, and drive success in today’s dynamic work landscape, as well as helping teams to achieve their goals. 
BinarBase: Bratislava-based BinarBase empowers enterprises to streamline their data analytics workflow and make informed decisions effortlessly. Founded in 2022, BinarBase secured €400k to offer a seamless and visually appealing solution to simplify data management and enhance business management capabilities. 
CloseRocket:   Founded in 2023, Closerocket is a Bratislava-based startup offering a B2B sales platform to help companies connect with sales talent, providing essential tools for scaling sales efforts. Recently securing €1 million in funding, Closerocket is poised to revolutionise business scalability with its innovative approach. 
DimensionLab: Kosice-based DimensionLab specialises in AI Engineering software. Their flagship product, Siml.ai drastically reduces simulation times by harnessing physics-informed neural networks. Founded in 2021, the company raised €600k to drive cost savings and accelerate the development of critical technologies like nuclear fusion, battery innovation, and advanced robotics. 
Seerlinq: Bratislava-based Seerlinq leverages advanced machine learning PPG analysis and medical expertise to elevate non-invasive diagnostics, disease monitoring, and treatment optimisation. Founded in 2022, Seerlinq is on a mission to personalise cardiovascular disease diagnostics and treatment by reinventing PPG curve analysis through artificial intelligence. 
Sensible Biotechnologies: Bratislava-based Sensible Biotechnologies is a biotechnology company that builds a cell-based platform for the production of high-quality mRNA for the next generation of therapeutics and vaccines. Founded in 2021, the startup raised €3.8 million to accelerate the development of its cell-based platform. 
Veles Farming: Founded in 2021, Veles Farming pioneers sustainable crop production by merging vertical farming with smart technologies, exemplified by their premier product, “Red Gold Saffron.” Bratislava-based Veles raised €740k to spearhead a transformative movement towards efficient and sustainable agriculture, inviting others to join in shaping the future of farming. 
NIVY Watch: Launched in 2022, NIVY Watch offers a smartwatch which seamlessly connects with Microsoft D365 Business Central, enhancing connectivity for deskless workers. This Bratislava-based startup raised €450k aiming to help deskless workers stay connected and synchronised with their company’s workflow. 
CulturePulse: Bratislava-based CulturePulse pioneers AI-driven decision-making with ARES, revolutionising global business strategies. Specialising in crafting psychologically authentic digital twins of their audience, the startup raised €1 million since its 2021 inception to minimize the risk of negative reactions and miscommunications.

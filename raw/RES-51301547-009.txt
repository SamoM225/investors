365.fintech Introduction
Hi there! Thanks a lot for your interest in 365.fintech. 

We are a boutique VC focusing on pre-seed & seed fintech. We invest in EU; with particular focus on the broader CEE & Baltics. We love API-first, infrastructural plays in b2b and b2b2c fintech but also do not stray away from more traditional financial services business models and b2b SaaS.

We have previously invested in amazing fintech companies as well as some of the best Fintech VC investors in the EU such as Speedinvest and Finch Capital.

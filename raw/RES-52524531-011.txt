Venture Stories 
DNA ERA 
The Slovak biotechnology company specializes in direct genetic analysis of DNA. Its technology can detect 74 carrier diseases and 34 health risks, as well as predispositions for certain sports and optimal nutrition. In 2021, DNA ERA received investment worth 250,000 € from the CB Growth One fund managed by CB Investment Management. DNA ERA used the funds to expand domestically and abroad. After experiencing rapid growth, they raised another 1 million € in collaboration with Crowdberry.

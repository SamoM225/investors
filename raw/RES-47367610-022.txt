About
BeiT enables the smart management of all financial transactions in residential and commercial buildings by migrating relevant data into a single platform. Utility suppliers, condominium and property managers, and tenants interact within one digital ecosystem. BeiT’s system becomes the single source of information for managing housing costs, administration, and maintenance for all properties in their customers’ portfolios.
